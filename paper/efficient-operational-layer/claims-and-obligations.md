# Claims and obligations for the efficient operational layer

This file is the manuscript's claim ledger. “Proved” means that a proof follows
from the definitions stated in the dossier or the paper and is written there.
“Conditional” names the exact missing premise. “Proposed” means that the item
is part of the recommended model but has not been reduced to a complete
mathematical proof. Literature facts are attributed rather than presented as
new results.

## Highest-priority open obligations

1. Compare the selected standard-Borel maximal-transcript carrier with any
   independently mandated external random-system carrier whose nonresponse or
   feedback convention differs. The finite common-domain presentation already
   embeds.
2. Instantiate further application-specific unbounded-output oracle APIs as
   needed. O11 already supplies one complete handle, scheduling, local-profile,
   and tail calculation; unrelated response laws require their own tails.
3. Develop automated or application-specific methods for finding the
   polynomial post-fixed points required by Q9. The generic
   response-adaptive constructor theorem is already proved, but candidate
