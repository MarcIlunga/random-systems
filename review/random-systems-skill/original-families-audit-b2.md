## Executive result

The frozen references contain a useful proof-routing vocabulary, but they are not safe as a factual guide to the current tree without revision. The highest-risk defects are:

1. The H-technique axes are presented as orthogonal in a way the library does not implement. In particular, `extended` has only equality-on-good and ratio-on-good endpoints (plus one filtered ratio endpoint), not all five analyses.
2. `selectHTechnique` and `#h_grammar` are not defined by this repository. They live only in the sibling `../ccprover` tree. The sibling selector does enumerate 15 of 60 combinations.
3. `compressedQuery_bound` is only a cubic numeric-bound monotonicity lemma. It is not the exact repeated-query normalization theorem and cannot by itself replace a query vector by its distinct-entry vector.
4. `optimal_probability_coupling_exists` couples the two supplied probability laws with disagreement equal to their raw static distance `δ`. It does not say that a coupling of arbitrary random-system representatives has disagreement equal to class distance or adaptive advantage. The latter conclusion is available only through the finite-input, common-domain, bounded-depth attainment endpoint.
5. The frozen winnability formula `Δ ≤ ν(S^A)` is not Theorem 2.37. The source and intended Lean theorem say `ν(S^A) = ω(S^A)` with attainment. Moreover, the current `GameWinnability.lean` does not elaborate in a focused build.
6. `CBCStructureGraph.lean` does not contain a completed counting engine. Its headline counting lemma has `sorry`, its own comment says the displayed second-term constant is not supported by the stated descriptor union, and the current file has additional elaboration failures.
7. The legacy general fundamental theorem and amplification theorem are not axiom-free: their proof paths contain `sorry`. The current legacy checks are additionally blocked by missing dependency oleans.
8. The strict-metric inequality is unconditional only with respect to a shared-domain assumption; it still requires normalized laws. Shared-domain equality is a general theorem, while the frozen reference cites the narrower `filterDom` corollary as if it were the general receipt. “Deletion rewind” is incorrect: rejection is observed, does not advance the system state, and the environment may continue.

## Verdict vocabulary

- **VERIFIED** — supported by a rendered primary source or by a current declaration that passed a focused check.

- **FALSE** — contradicted by the primary source, current signature, current source, or focused check.
- **STALE** — the named object remains meaningful, but a location, namespace, availability statement, or build-status claim is out of date.
- **UNVERIFIED** — no adequate primary/current receipt was available, including empirical superlatives and checks blocked by the current dependency state.

## Recommended minimum rewrites

The following replacements remove the most dangerous failure modes without changing the references' overall teaching style.

### H-technique availability

> Five ordinary fixed-query analysis shapes are available: perfect ratio, equality-on-good, ratio-on-good, expectation, and partition. The library's endpoint surface is sparse across transcript model and filter choices; do not infer a theorem from the naming grammar. In this repository, search the live declarations. The sibling `ccprover` repository optionally provides `selectHTechnique` and `#h_grammar`, which enumerate 15 of 60 combinations.

### Query compression

> Exact repeated-query compression is expressed by the `fTransform_*_expand_compressedQuery` equalities in `RandomSystems/QueryCompression.lean`; `compressedQuery_injective` proves the compressed vector is injective. `compressedQuery_card_le` controls its length. `compressedQuery_bound` is only the specialized implication from `q³ ≤ N²` to the corresponding compressed-cardinality bound. The `htechnique_compress` tactic rewrites only registered SoP-shaped transcript laws.

### Coupling

> For supplied normalized finite-support laws, `optimal_probability_coupling_exists` constructs a normalized joint whose disagreement equals their raw static distance `δ`. For random-system advantage, tight representatives and an optimal joint are currently proved on the modern surface under finite input alphabet, one common atom domain, and a uniform query-depth bound by `exists_equivalent_representatives_with_probability_coupling_disagreement_eq_optimal_advantage_of_finite_common_domain_and_bounded`. An arbitrary user-constructed coupling can still be loose.

### Winnability

> Thesis Theorem 2.37 is `ν(S^A)=ω(S^A)` with attainment, not a generic `Δ≤ν(S^A)` theorem. The intended repository endpoint is `winnability_theorem_of_fixed_domain_and_bounded`, but the current `GameWinnability.lean` does not build and must not be cited until its focused check passes.

### Structure graphs

> `CBCStructureGraph.lean` currently contains partial Jha–Nandi structure-graph infrastructure and a tolerant conditional-equivalence bridge. The headline counting lemma `mass_cbcGraphBad_le` is admitted, its displayed constant is questioned by the file's own comment, and the current module has additional elaboration failures. Do not cite the downstream linear-in-`L` theorem as proved.

### Metric receipt

> For normalized laws, `StrictContextAdvantage.maxEDist_le_maxAdvantage` gives `maxEDist ≤ ENNReal.ofReal Δ` without a shared-domain premise. For normalized laws with one common support-atom domain, `StrictContextSharedDomain.maxEDist_eq_ofReal_maxAdvantage_of_sharedDomain` gives equality. The narrower `maxEDist_filterDom_eq_ofReal_maxAdvantage` applies to same-predicate restrictions of total laws. Rejection is observed skip semantics, not rewind.
